# Case IQ 10-second commercial: critique rubric

## What was intended
`brief.md` (in this folder) is the full spec. In short:

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 10.0 s, for social feeds. Sound on first, but it must also work muted.

**One idea:** the page that shows what happened is somewhere in the pile. Case IQ puts the pile in order and finds it.

**Message match:** the voiceover is the site's hero headline word for word, "You know what happened. Let’s find the page that shows it." The end-card line is the site's S4 heading, "Bring the pile. Leave with a timeline.", and the button is the site's main call to action, "Get my case in order".

**Timeline (seconds):**
- **0–2.2:** a night kitchen table, seen from above, buried in papers. Hands search through them. Bubble 1 and the voiceover say "You know what happened."
- **2.2–2.9:** the hands stop, the sheets lift and hang in the air, and the music cuts to a held breath.
- **2.9–6.3:** the sheets glide into one straight column down the table. Bubble 2 and the voiceover say "Let’s find the page that shows it."
- **6.3–7.6:** a fingertip rests on one page, and a page tag ("p. 47") pops onto it.
- **7.6–10:** the brand ground rises and the bubbles stay. The wordmark, the line and the indigo send pill arrive, and the voiceover says "Case IQ."

## Scoring
- **Scale:** score each of your four dimensions from 1 to 10.
- **Exit bar:** every dimension at 8 or above, and zero blocking issues.
- **Blocking issues:**
  - anything that would make a viewer scroll past in the first 2 s;
  - anything that would make them misunderstand what Case IQ is, or distrust it;
  - anything that breaks a brand or honesty rule;
  - any AI artifact visible at a normal glance (extra or fused fingers, paper that morphs, gibberish that reads as words, warped objects);
  - any visual defect visible at a normal glance.
- **Fixes:** for every point under 8, give the concrete fix: which asset (keyframe, video prompt, overlay, end card, audio) and exactly what to change. Rank fixes by their effect on a stressed viewer's chance of tapping through.
- **Length:** keep praise to one line, and report in under 400 words.

### Critic A: creative director (captivation and craft)
- **A1 Hook:** would the first 3 s stop a thumb in a TikTok or Reels feed at 11 pm? Is frame 1 striking, and is the problem readable by 2 s?
- **A2 Story and pacing:** does it run problem, turn, payoff, brand in 10 s with one idea? Does the magic beat land? Is there dead air or crowding?
- **A3 Visual craft:** AI artifacts, how physically believable the paper motion is, composition, light, and how well the overlays sit in the picture (type, bubbles, tag, end card, transitions).
- **A4 Memorability and brand:** a day later, would you remember who this was for and what it does? Does it feel like Case IQ? Does the end card do its job?

### Critic B: Maria (the target viewer)
**Persona:**
- **Who:** Maria, 41, lives in Hamilton, Ontario. Her employer told her in January that her job was safe, then let her go.
- **Right now:** it's 11 pm. She's on her phone with a folder of screenshots and one bar of patience.
- **Wary of:** AI, websites that take her details, and the cost.
- **Approach:** stay in character and say what you would actually do.

**Dimensions:**
- **B1 Stop and recognition:** does she stop scrolling, and does she see herself?
- **B2 Understanding:** after 10 s, can she say in her own words what Case IQ does?
- **B3 Trust and tone:** is it calm or salesy? Does the paper magic read as hype or as AI over-promising? Does anything feel off?
- **B4 Action:** would she tap? What is the next question in her head?
