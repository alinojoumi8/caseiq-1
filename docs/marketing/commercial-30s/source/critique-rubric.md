## Scoring
- **Scale:** score each of your four dimensions from 1 to 10.
- **Exit bar:** every dimension at 8 or above, and zero blocking issues.
- **Blocking issues:**
  - anything that would make a viewer scroll past in the first 2 s, or leave before 6 s;
  - anything that would make them misunderstand what Case IQ is, or distrust it;
  - anything that breaks a brand or honesty rule;
  - any AI artifact visible at a normal glance (extra or fused fingers, paper that visibly melts or smears, objects that pop or duplicate, gibberish that reads as words);
  - any visual defect visible at a normal glance (a flash, overlap, clipped text, text outside the safe zone).
- **Fixes:** for every point under 8, give the concrete fix: which asset (overlay, edit, audio, end card, or a footage retake) and exactly what to change. Rank fixes by their effect on a stressed viewer's chance of tapping through.
- **Length:** keep praise to one line, and report in under 450 words.

### Critic A: creative director (captivation and craft)
- **A1 Hook:** would the first 3 s stop a thumb at 11 pm? Is frame 1 striking? Is the problem readable by 2 s?
- **A2 Story and pacing over 30 s:** does it hold through the 6 s and 15 s marks? Does the turn land, and does the timeline beat earn its 8 s? Is there dead air, crowding or a sag?
- **A3 Visual craft:**
  - AI artifacts, and how physically believable the paper is;
  - composition and light;
  - how well the overlays sit in the picture (bubbles over the night and morning shots, the timeline panel, the end card, the transitions).
- **A4 Memorability and brand:** a day later, would you remember who this was for and what it does? Does it feel like Case IQ and like the 10-second spot's campaign? Does the end card do its job?

### Critic B: Maria (the target viewer)
**Persona:**
- **Who:** Maria, 41, lives in Hamilton, Ontario. Her employer told her in January that her job was safe, then let her go.
- **Right now:** it's 11 pm. She's on her phone with a folder of screenshots and one bar of patience.
- **Wary of:** AI, websites that take her details, and the cost.
- **Approach:** stay in character and say what you would actually do, second by second if it helps.

**Dimensions:**
- **B1 Stop and recognition:** does she stop scrolling, and does she see herself?
- **B2 Understanding:** after 30 s, can she say in her own words what Case IQ does and what she gets?
- **B3 Trust and tone:** is it calm or salesy? Does the paper magic or the example timeline read as hype or as AI over-promising? Does anything feel off?
- **B4 Action:** would she tap? What is the next question in her head?
